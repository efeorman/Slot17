# Legal information

**Status:** general project information, not legal advice.

## What Slot17 is

Slot17 is software for the **passive detection of certain properties of TETRA radio signals** with supported
receiver hardware. It is meant for technical observation and research. According to its design documents,
Slot17 does not transmit TETRA signals and does not decode message content or speech.

A detection **does not confirm the presence, identity, location, direction or distance of a police,
ambulance or fire-service vehicle**. The values shown are properties of received radio signals; it does
not follow that an emergency vehicle is nearby or approaching.
